## Findings and response

| ID | Severity | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| M-1 | Medium | A buy's quote left out the protocol fee taken during the buy's own accrual, so buys at the exact quote (every AgentRouter `buyFor` / `shieldFor`) reverted with `Slippage` | Fixed: `EngineCore.quoteNav` takes the same fee off the quote (same amount and rounding as `reportFunding` / `takeIncome`), for buy and sell quotes. PoC `test/audit/EngineQuoteFundingFee.t.sol`: 2 of 3 fail before (48,557,744 != 48,557,738), all pass after |
| M-2 | Medium | Refilling a short Uniswap observation ring erased the worse-of trade TWAP, so weekend or halt sells settled at the stale last-good price | Fixed: `PriceGuard.tradeTwap` falls back to the longest window the ring still covers (never spot); `tradeTwapLost` reports a set but unservable source; sells then escrow, queued fills wait, and LP entries and exits pause (engine liability and pool both fail closed). `SystemChecks` fails a pool below 2,160 observations. PoCs `OracleTwapRingErase.t.sol` (fails before: tradeTwap 0) and `TwapLostFailsClosed.t.sol` |
| L-1 | Low | A claim reverted with arithmetic underflow when only the Reserve kept NAV positive | Fixed: `need = target > slice ? target - slice : 0`. PoC `PoolClaimReserveUnderflow.t.sol`: panic 0x11 before, pays after |
| L-2 | Low | A claim's hedge-sale shortfall was charged at max(S, TWAP) though the claim was valued at min(S, TWAP) | Fixed: the shortfall is measured at the valuation price; the sale floor keeps the higher one |

Found while fixing M-2: `WriterPool._navLiability` swallowed any engine revert and fell back to book value. It now passes
a reason-carrying revert through (only an engine without the call falls back).

## Upgrade

The fixes go live through the 48 h timelock as ONE batch: `upgradeToAndCall` on the engine and on the WriterPool (new
implementations and their changed libraries, deployed by `contracts/script/AuditUpgrade.s.sol`). Proven on a mainnet
fork by `contracts/script/fork/audit-upgrade-proof.sh`:
- execute is refused before 48 h and succeeds after;
- the implementation slots switch, and roles and caps are unchanged;
- storage layout is identical, and PostDeployCheck passes afterwards.

Gas: deploy 33.05M, schedule 67k, execute 89k.

PriceGuard is not redeployed. The engine and pool detect a lost TWAP from `twapSource` and `tradeTwap`, which the live
PriceGuard has, so the fail-closed rules work with it. Its pools hold 7,200 observations (2 h at one per second), so a
dust refill cannot empty the 5- or 30-minute window. The longest-window fallback and `tradeTwapLost` in `PriceGuard.sol`
are for the next PriceGuard deployment. The deployed PriceGuard therefore matches the deployment commit (tag it), not
this one.
