# Security policy

Lemniscate is pre-release software and has not been audited. Do not use it with funds you cannot lose.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately and do not open a public issue. Contact details will be published with the first deployment.
Include the affected contract or service, steps to reproduce, and the impact you see.

## Scope

Contracts under `contracts/src` and services under `app/src`. Third-party contracts (Stock Tokens, Chainlink feeds, Uniswap,
USDG) and vendored libraries in `contracts/lib` are out of scope.

## Status

Roles, trust assumptions, accepted risks and audit history will be documented here before any deployment.
